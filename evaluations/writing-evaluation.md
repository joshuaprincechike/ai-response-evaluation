# AI Writing Evaluation

## Evaluation Focus

Assessment of AI-generated writing for clarity, structure, tone, grammar, audience alignment, and instruction following.

## Key Criteria

- Clarity
- Grammar and mechanics
- Tone
- Organization
- Conciseness
- Audience alignment
- Instruction following
- Meaning preservation

## Evaluation Approach

Responses are compared against the original writing objective to determine which version communicates the intended message most effectively.

## Skills Demonstrated

Writing evaluation, editing, proofreading, communication analysis, audience assessment, and comparative response evaluation.
