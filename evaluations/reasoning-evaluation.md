# AI Reasoning Evaluation

## Evaluation Focus

Assessment of whether an AI response uses logical, coherent, and well-supported reasoning.

## Key Criteria

- Logical consistency
- Evidence and assumptions
- Step-by-step reasoning quality
- Relevance
- Conclusion support
- Internal consistency

## Evaluation Approach

The reasoning is examined to determine whether the conclusion follows from the available information and whether important assumptions or gaps affect the result.

## Common Issues

- Unsupported conclusions
- Contradictory reasoning
- Missing assumptions
- Logical gaps
- Incorrect interpretation of evidence

## Skills Demonstrated

Logical analysis, critical reasoning, comparative evaluation, assumption analysis, and evidence-based assessment.
