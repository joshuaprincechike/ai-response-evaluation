# AI Fact-Checking & Hallucination Detection

## Evaluation Focus

Assessment of factual accuracy, unsupported claims, technical precision, and appropriate qualification of AI-generated information.

## Key Criteria

- Factual accuracy
- Evidence and verification
- Technical precision
- Unsupported claims
- Internal consistency
- Appropriate qualification

## Evaluation Approach

Claims are examined against reliable reference information where verification is required. Particular attention is given to overgeneralizations, fabricated details, and conclusions that are not adequately supported.

## Skills Demonstrated

Fact-checking, hallucination detection, claim verification, technical analysis, critical reasoning, and evidence-based evaluation.
