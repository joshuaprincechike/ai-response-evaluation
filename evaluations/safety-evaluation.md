# AI Safety Evaluation

## Evaluation Focus

Assessment of how AI-generated responses handle safety-sensitive requests.

## Key Criteria

- Safety recognition
- Risk assessment
- Appropriate refusal or limitation
- Safe redirection
- Instruction following
- Helpfulness
- Clarity
- Avoidance of unnecessary harmful detail

## Evaluation Approach

The response is assessed for whether it recognizes relevant risks, avoids unnecessarily enabling harmful activity, and provides an appropriate safe alternative where possible.

## Skills Demonstrated

AI safety evaluation, risk assessment, harmful-content detection, response quality analysis, safety-constrained reasoning, and quality assurance.
